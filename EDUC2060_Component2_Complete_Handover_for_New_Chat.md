## 3. Component 2 — Step 1: EXACT baseline draft, Multistructural C

# Component 2 — Assessment Task Proposal

**Syllabus topic:** The business environment

**Stage and year:** Stage 5, Year 9 Commerce

**Assessment mode:** Individual written business advisory report (approximately 700–900 words)

**Assessed syllabus outcomes (NESA Commerce 7–10, 2024):**

- **CO5-ECB-01:** explains economic and business concepts and processes.
- **CO5-DEC-01:** investigates and assesses factors affecting decisions.
- **CO5-PRO-01:** evaluates and applies options for solving problems and addressing issues.
- **CO5-COM-01:** selects and uses a range of information sources and terminology to communicate concepts and ideas.

**Proposed task: Improving a Small Business**

Students act as advisers to a hypothetical local café that is experiencing customer-service complaints, staffing difficulties and increasing operating costs. They receive a teacher-prepared case pack containing a business profile, customer feedback, a staffing roster, three applicant summaries, sales information and an outline of a possible AI customer-service or stock-management tool. Students may also draw on evidence gathered during the unit’s local-business site study.

Students submit an advisory report with three short sections. First, they identify the café’s main performance problem and explain how staffing and service affect its operations. Second, they recommend a suitable employee from the candidate profiles, using the supplied selection criteria. Third, they compare two practical improvements, including whether the café should trial the proposed AI tool, and recommend a course of action using evidence from the case pack.

The report should explain at least two likely consequences for the café, its employees or customers. Students must use relevant Commerce terminology and refer to information supplied in the case materials. A simple report structure and source checklist will be provided. Students will be assessed on their understanding of business processes, use of evidence, quality of recommendations and clarity of communication.

The task consolidates the revised recruitment simulation, local-business inquiry and current-issues investigation into one assessment of business decision-making.

**Step 1 intent:** Suitable C-band starting proposal, not yet a claim of high-band achievement.

## 5. Component 2 — Step 3: EXACT further revised draft, Extended Abstract A + Prestructural F

# Component 2 — Assessment Task Proposal

**Syllabus topic:** The business environment  
**Stage/year:** Stage 5, Year 9 Commerce  
**Assessment mode:** Individual business advisory brief (600–750 words)  
**Audience:** The owner of a hypothetical local café

**Assessed syllabus outcomes (NESA Commerce 7–10, 2024):**

- **CO5-ECB-01:** explains economic and business concepts and processes.
- **CO5-DEC-01:** investigates and assesses factors affecting decisions.
- **CO5-PRO-01:** evaluates and applies options for solving problems and addressing issues.
- **CO5-COM-01:** selects and uses a range of information sources and terminology to communicate concepts and ideas.

**Proposed task: Improving a Small Business**

Students advise the owner of a café facing customer-service complaints, staff shortages and rising costs. The teacher supplies a common case pack containing a business profile, sales trends, customer feedback, a staffing roster, three applicant profiles, and costed information about a possible AI tool. A fixed improvement budget prevents the café from pursuing every option. Students may apply lessons from their site study, but all essential assessment evidence is supplied.

Students prepare a brief addressed to the owner with three sections:

1. **Diagnose** the main performance problem using two pieces of evidence and explain its effect on operations.
2. **Recommend** a candidate for a staffing vacancy, comparing at least two applicants against relevant criteria and acknowledging a trade-off.
3. **Evaluate** two feasible responses to the café's problems, including whether to trial or defer the AI tool. Recommend an approach within budget, considering costs, customers and staff.

Students conclude by proposing one observable indicator for reviewing their recommendation and identifying an uncertainty in the evidence.

The teacher provides a report template, a worked example of evidence-based reasoning and shared success criteria covering evidence, judgement, feasibility and communication, without prescribing a 'correct' recommendation. All students use the same core case pack and submit individually; no site visit or AI software is required.

The assessment elicits business explanation (CO5-ECB-01), analysis of decision factors (CO5-DEC-01), evaluation of solutions (CO5-PRO-01) and clear evidence-based communication (CO5-COM-01). It integrates learning from the recruitment simulation, site-study inquiry and AI investigation in one authentic, manageable task.

**Step 3 revision rationale:**

- **Blue / A:** Authentic named audience, business-advisory brief format; plausible fixed budget and costed alternatives; defensible choices and trade-offs; observable indicator to evaluate success; student judgement without predetermined 'correct' option.
- **Red / F:** Shorten from 700–900 to 600–750 words for Year 9; define task sections and transparent success criteria; common pack, individual submissions and no compulsory excursion/AI tool; avoid unclear or inappropriate demands.
- **Grey / general:** More concise task phrasing and reordering.
